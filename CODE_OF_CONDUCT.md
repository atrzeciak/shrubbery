# Code of Conduct

This is a small project with one maintainer, so this is short.

## The standard

Be decent to people. Assume the person you are replying to is acting in good faith and knows things
you do not. Criticise the code, the decision or the documentation — not the person who wrote it.

Not welcome, in issues, pull requests, commit messages or anywhere else this project is discussed:
harassment, insults, discriminatory language, sexual attention, publishing anyone's private
information, or sustained disruption after being asked to stop.

The subject matter here is families, which means threads can touch names, dates, illnesses and
deaths. Treat what people mention about their own family as theirs, and do not repeat it elsewhere.

## Scope

Anywhere this project happens: issues, pull requests, discussions, commits, and private
correspondence about it.

## Enforcement

Report anything that needs reporting through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on the
**Security** tab, which is a private channel to the maintainer whatever the subject.

The maintainer will read it and decide, and the answers available are a word, an edit, a locked
thread, or a block. There is no committee and no appeal process. Reports stay private; the person
who reported will not be named without their agreement.

Being right about the code does not exempt anyone from this. Neither does maintaining it.
